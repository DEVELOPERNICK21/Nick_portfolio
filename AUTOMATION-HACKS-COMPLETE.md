## 🎯 FREE AUTOMATION TOOLS (Start Here!)

### 1. EMAIL AUTOMATION

#### Option A: Gmail Templates (100% FREE - Easiest)

**Setup (5 minutes):**
1. Open Gmail → Settings (gear icon) → See all settings
2. Go to "Advanced" tab
3. Enable "Templates"
4. Compose email → Write template → Three dots → "Templates" → "Save draft as template"

**Templates to Create:**
- Agency outreach (Template 1)
- Brand collaboration (Template 2)
- Tech company pitch (Template 3)
- Follow-up email
- Thank you email

**How to Use:**
- Compose new email
- Three dots → "Templates" → Select template
- Personalize (add name, specific details)
- Send!

**Hack:** Create variations for different types of agencies/brands

---

#### Option B: Mixmax (Email Tracking - FREE)

**Setup:**
1. Sign up at mixmax.com (free tier)
2. Connect Gmail
3. Install browser extension
4. Send emails with tracking enabled

**Free Tier:**
- 25 tracked emails/month
- See when emails are opened
- Know when links are clicked
- Schedule emails
- Templates

**Why It's Powerful:**
- Know if they opened your email (even if no reply)
- See if they clicked your portfolio link
- Know the best time to follow up
- Track which subject lines work best

**Hack:** Use tracking to know when to follow up (if opened but no reply after 3 days)

---

**Free Tier:**

- Hashtag suggestions
- Best time to post recommendations

- Visual planning (see your week/month)

- Analytics

**How to Use:**
1. Plan your week's content (Sunday)

### 3. CRM/TRACKING AUTOMATION

#### Option A: Google Sheets (100% FREE)

**Setup:**
1. Create new Google Sheet
2. Add columns: Date, Contact, Type, Email, Status, Follow-up, Notes
3. Use for tracking all outreach

**Template Columns:**
```
Date | Contact | Type | Email | Status | Follow-up | Response | Notes
```

**Hack:** Use color coding
- Green = Positive response
- Yellow = Follow-up needed
- Red = Not interested
- Blue = Pending

**Automation:**
- Use Google Sheets formulas to calculate follow-up dates
- Set up reminders in Google Calendar

---

### 2. EMAIL OUTREACH HACKS

#### Timing Hacks:
- **Best Days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best Times:** 10am-2pm (their timezone)
- **Avoid:** Monday mornings, Friday afternoons
- **Hack:** Use World Clock to know their timezone

#### Subject Line Hacks:
- Use numbers: "5 Reasons I'm Perfect for Your Brand"
- Create curiosity: "Model Who Understands Your Product"
- Personal: "Love Your Latest Campaign - Collaboration?"
- Question: "Looking for Tech-Savvy Models?"

**Hack:** A/B test subject lines, track which get responses

#### Follow-Up Hacks:
- **First follow-up:** 1 week later
- **Second follow-up:** 2 weeks later
- **Change subject line** each time
- **Add new value** (new work, updated portfolio)

**Hack:** Set calendar reminders for follow-ups

## 🚨 AUTOMATION DON'TS (Avoid These!)

❌ **Don't Auto-Like/Follow on Instagram**
- Can get you banned
- Looks spammy
- Doesn't build real relationships

❌ **Don't Send Generic Emails**
- Always personalize
- Automation should help, not replace personalization

❌ **Don't Spam**
- Respect rate limits
- Don't send too many emails at once
- Space out your outreach

❌ **Don't Ignore Responses**
- Reply to every response
- Build relationships
- Follow through on commitments
