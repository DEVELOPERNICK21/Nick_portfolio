## 🎯 Quick Tips for Outreach:

1. **Personalize Every Message** - Mention something specific about them
2. **Keep It Concise** - Busy people appreciate brevity
3. **Include Clear CTA** - What do you want them to do?
4. **Make It Easy** - Include all links upfront
5. **Follow Up** - But don't be pushy (1-2 follow-ups max)
6. **Be Professional** - But also show personality
7. **Proofread** - Typos kill credibility
