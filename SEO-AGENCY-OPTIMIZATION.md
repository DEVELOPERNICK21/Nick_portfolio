### 2. **Enhanced Structured Data (Schema.org)**

#### Multiple Schema Types Implemented:

**1. Person Schema** (Enhanced)
- Added `worksFor` property linking to CastYou agency
- Expanded `knowsAbout` with more expertise areas
- Better description for agency discovery

**2. ProfessionalService Schema** (NEW)
- Defines modeling as a professional service
- Lists service types: Fashion, Editorial, Commercial, Brand Ambassador, etc.
- Includes service area (Worldwide)
- Links to contact page for bookings

**3. FAQPage Schema** (NEW)
- Answers common agency questions:
  - "Is Nikhil Kubde available for modeling bookings?"
  - "What types of modeling projects does he work on?"
  - "How can agencies book him?"
  - "What makes him unique as a model?"
