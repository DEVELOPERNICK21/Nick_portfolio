# 🚀 Quick Start: Get Opportunities in 7 Days

## Day 1: Setup (2 hours)

### Morning (1 hour):
1. **Update Instagram Bio** (15 min)
   - Use template from `INSTAGRAM-BIO-SUGGESTIONS.md`
   - Make it professional and clear

2. **Create Email Templates** (30 min)
   - Copy templates from `OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md`
   - Customize with your info
   - Save in Gmail templates

3. **Set Up Tracking** (15 min)
   - Create Google Sheet
   - Add columns: Name, Email, Type, Date, Status
   - Add 10 agencies/brands to start

### Afternoon (1 hour):
1. **Research 20 Agencies** (30 min)
   - Google: "modeling agencies [your city]"
   - Find email addresses
   - Add to tracking sheet

2. **Research 20 Brands** (30 min)
   - Find brands you like on Instagram
   - Check their websites for contact info
   - Add to tracking sheet

---

## Day 2-3: First Outreach (2 hours)

### Send 10 Emails:
- 5 to agencies (use Template 1)
- 5 to brands (use Template 2)
- Personalize each one
- Track in spreadsheet

### Post on Instagram:
- Upload your best photo
- Use hashtags from `OPPORTUNITY-STRATEGY.md`
- Engage with 20 accounts

---

## Day 4-5: Expand (2 hours)

### Send 15 More Emails:
- 10 more agencies
- 5 photographers (use Template 3)
- Follow up on any responses

### Instagram:
- Post another photo
- Engage daily
- Reply to all comments/DMs

---

## Day 6-7: Follow-Up (1 hour)

### Follow Up:
- Check emails sent on Day 2-3
- Send follow-up to non-responders
- Thank anyone who responded

### Plan Next Week:
- Review what worked
- Adjust strategy
- Plan content for next week

## 💡 Pro Tips for Success:

1. **Be Consistent** - Small daily actions > Big weekly actions
2. **Personalize** - Generic emails get ignored
3. **Follow Up** - Most opportunities come from follow-ups
4. **Stay Professional** - Always be respectful
5. **Track Everything** - Know what's working
