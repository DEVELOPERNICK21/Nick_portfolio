# Automation Setup Guide for Modeling Career

## 🎯 Quick Start: Free Automation Tools

### 1. Email Automation (FREE)

#### Option A: Gmail Templates (Easiest - FREE)

**Setup:**
1. Open Gmail
2. Click Settings (gear icon) → See all settings
3. Go to "Advanced" tab
4. Enable "Templates"
5. Compose email → Write template → Click three dots → "Templates" → "Save draft as template"

**How to Use:**
- Compose new email
- Click three dots → "Templates" → Select your template
- Personalize and send

**Templates to Create:**
- Agency outreach
- Brand collaboration
- Photographer TFP
- Follow-up email

---

#### Option B: Mailchimp (FREE up to 500 contacts)

**Setup:**
1. Sign up at mailchimp.com (free)
2. Create audience/list
3. Add contacts manually or import CSV
4. Create email campaign
5. Schedule or send immediately

**Best For:**
- Newsletter to interested parties
- Follow-up sequences
- Tracking opens/clicks

**Limitations:**
- Free tier: 500 contacts, 1,000 sends/month
- Good for starting out

---

#### Option C: Mixmax (Email Tracking - FREE tier)

**Setup:**
1. Sign up at mixmax.com
2. Connect Gmail
3. Install browser extension
4. Send emails with tracking enabled

**Features:**
- See when emails are opened
- Know when links are clicked
- Schedule emails
- Templates

**Free Tier:**
- 25 tracked emails/month
- Perfect for starting out

---

### 2. Instagram Post Scheduling (FREE)

#### Option A: Later (Best Free Option)

**Setup:**

2. Connect Instagram account
3. Upload photos

**How to Use:**
1. Plan your week's content
2. Upload all photos
3. Write captions with hashtags
4. Schedule for optimal times
5. Review and publish

### 4. Tracking & CRM (FREE)

#### Option A: Google Sheets (Simplest)

**Create Spreadsheet:**
```
Columns:
- Contact Name
- Company/Agency
- Email
- Date Contacted
- Status (Sent/Replied/Follow-up)
- Follow-up Date
- Notes
- Response
```

**Automation:**
- Use conditional formatting for status
- Set reminders in Google Calendar
- Use formulas to calculate days since contact

**Template Formula:**
```
=IF(TODAY()>E2+7, "Follow Up", "Wait")
```
(E2 = Date Contacted column)

---

#### Option B: Airtable (Better Organization - FREE)

**Setup:**
1. Sign up at airtable.com
2. Create base "Model Outreach"
3. Create tables:
   - Agencies
   - Brands

**Free Tier:**

**Features:**
- Better than spreadsheets
- Link related records
- Multiple views
- Calendar view for follow-ups

---

#### Option C: Notion (All-in-One - FREE)

**Setup:**
1. Sign up at notion.so
2. Create workspace
3. Create database for contacts
4. Create pages for strategy, templates, etc.

**Free Tier:**
- Unlimited pages

- Personal use

## 🤖 Advanced Automation (When Ready)

### 1. Zapier Automation (FREE tier available)

**What It Does:**
- Connects different apps
- Automates workflows

**Example Automations:**
- New Instagram post → Auto-post to Twitter
- New email in inbox → Add to tracking sheet
- Calendar event → Send reminder email

**Free Tier:**
- 100 tasks/month
- 5 Zaps (automations)

**Setup Example:**
1. Sign up at zapier.com
2. Create Zap
3. Trigger: New Instagram post
4. Action: Add row to Google Sheets
5. Test and activate

---

### 2. IFTTT (If This Then That) - FREE

**Similar to Zapier but simpler**

**Useful Automations:**
- Instagram post → Auto-post to Facebook
- New email → Add to spreadsheet
- Calendar reminder → Send email

**Free Tier:**
- Unlimited applets
- Good for simple automations

## 🚨 Automation Don'ts

### ❌ Don't Automate:
- Personal responses (always personalize)
- Engagement (likes/comments) - can get banned
- Follow/unfollow - Instagram hates this
- Generic messages - always customize

### ✅ Safe to Automate:
- Post scheduling
- Email templates (but personalize!)
- Content creation
- Tracking and organization
- Reminders and follow-ups
