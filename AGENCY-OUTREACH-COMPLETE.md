## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Always Personalize** - Mention something specific about their work/brand/agency
2. **Lead with Your Modeling Skills** - Focus on fashion, editorial, commercial experience
3. **Keep It Concise** - Busy people appreciate brevity
4. **Include Portfolio Link** - Make it easy for them to see your work
5. **Follow Up Strategically** - 1-2 follow-ups max, spaced 1-2 weeks apart
6. **Track Everything** - Know who you contacted and when
7. **Be Professional but Personable** - Show personality and enthusiasm
8. **Proofread Everything** - Typos kill credibility
9. **Show Versatility** - Mention different types of work you can do
10. **Highlight Your Growth** - Show you're actively building your portfolio
