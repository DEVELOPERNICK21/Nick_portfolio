## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Always Personalize** - Mention something specific about them
2. **Lead with Your Unique Value** - Tech background is your differentiator
3. **Keep It Concise** - Busy people appreciate brevity
4. **Include Portfolio Link** - Make it easy for them
5. **Follow Up Strategically** - 1-2 follow-ups max, spaced 1-2 weeks apart
6. **Track Everything** - Know who you contacted and when
7. **Be Professional but Personable** - Show personality
8. **Proofread Everything** - Typos kill credibility
