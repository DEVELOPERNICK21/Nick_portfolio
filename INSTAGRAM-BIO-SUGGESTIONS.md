## Instagram Bio Best Practices:

1. **Use Line Breaks**: Make it easy to read
2. **Include Contact**: Email for bookings
3. **Add Location**: Helps with local opportunities
4. **Show Versatility**: Mention different types of work
5. **Use Emojis Sparingly**: 2-3 emojis max for professionalism
6. **Include Link**: Link to your portfolio or CastYou profile
7. **Update Regularly**: Keep it current with your latest work
